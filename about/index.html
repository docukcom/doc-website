About DOC

An independent company building an AI helper for people waiting for NHS care in England.

The name

“Doc” is a doctor, and it is a document. The name stands for both: getting to the right specialist, and handling the letters along the way. Our website is doc.uk.com.

Who is building it

Andrii Nikolyn

Founder, AI product engineer

Andrii has built AI products at Faculty and AI systems at Darktrace. He holds a first-class MEng in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence from the University of Southampton.

Independent

DOC is not part of the NHS and is not endorsed by it. We mention the NHS only to describe the services we help people with, and we do not use NHS logos (NHS Identity guidelines).
