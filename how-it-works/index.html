Six steps from waiting to moving

This is the service we are building. It is in development, and the first version will cover England only.

  1. 1. Tell your story

    Write it the way you would tell a friend: where your GP referred you, when, and what has happened since. DOC asks a follow-up question only when something important is missing.

  2. 2. See your rights

    DOC checks your situation against the NHS choice rules for England: whether you can choose, which exceptions apply, and what to ask for. When it is not sure, it says so and tells you who to ask.

  3. 3. Compare the options

    For your specialty, DOC lists hospitals and clinics that work with the NHS, with typical waits from official data and the distance from home. Sorted by wait and distance only.

  4. 4. Get your letters

    A request to your GP, a reminder, or a complaint if a referral seems lost. You edit it and send it yourself.

  5. 5. Track the referral

    DOC keeps a timeline of every letter, call and reply. When nothing happens for too long, it tells you, and suggests what to do next.

  6. 6. Understand your letters

    Specialist letters are written for other doctors. DOC gives you a short summary in plain words, in your language, and questions for your next appointment. It adds no medical advice.
