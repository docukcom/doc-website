Write to us

AI that drafts. You decide.

DOC combines a language model with a rule base and official NHS data. The AI writes and explains. The rules and the data decide what is true.

Three parts, one answer

  1. Language model

    Reads your story in your own words, asks what is missing, drafts letters and turns specialist letters into plain language.

  2. Rule base

    NHS choice rules for England, written down as checks the model has to pass. Each rule links to its official source.

  3. Waiting time data

    Official NHS England statistics by specialty and provider, refreshed when new figures are published.

Where the AI stops

It does

  • Explain rules and options
  • Draft letters you can edit
  • Summarise letters in plain words
  • Remind you when to follow up

It never does

  • Assess symptoms
  • Suggest a diagnosis or treatment
  • Send letters or make calls for you
  • Choose a clinic for you

DOC does not assess symptoms or give medical advice, so we do not expect it to be a medical device. We will confirm this with a specialist before launch (MHRA guidance).

Your data

  • Health information is special category data. UK GDPR gives it extra protection, and so will we (ICO guidance).
  • We ask only for what a step needs. A postcode for distance, a referral date for the timeline.
  • You can delete your account and your data. At any time, in one step.
  • No selling, no advertising. Your data is not shared with clinics or employers.
  • Before launch we will register with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data regulator, and publish a full privacy notice for the service.

Building health AI with real guardrails is the hard part. If that is your field, we would like to talk.
