Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. This policy covers this website only. The DOC service will have its own privacy notice before launch.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Andrii Nikolyn, founder of DOC. The company is not yet registered. When it is, this policy will be updated with the company’s name and address. Contact: hello@doc.uk.com.

What this website collects

Nothing by itself. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. Fonts and all other files are served from this website.

The contact form does not send anything. It prepares an email in your own mail app. We only receive your details if you choose to send that email.

Emails you send us

We use your name, email address and message to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages, and your consent where you give it in the form. We keep emails for as long as the conversation is useful, and for no more than 24 months after the last message, unless the law requires longer. Please do not send medical records or details of your health by email.

Hosting

Like any website, the hosting provider may keep short-term server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the service secure. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and take a copy. Write to hello@doc.uk.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We will update this page when the company is registered and before the DOC service launches.

DOC is an independent service in development. It is not part of the NHS and is not endorsed by it.
