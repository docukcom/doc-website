You can choose where you are referred

In England, if your GP refers you for non-urgent, consultant-led care, you can usually choose the hospital or clinic.

When the right applies

“This choice is a legal right.”

NHS website, About patient choice

AI that drafts. You decide.

DOC reads your situation, checks it against the rules and writes. It does not judge your health.

What DOC does

  • Understands your situation from your own words
  • Matches it with NHS choice rules for England
  • Reads official waiting time data
  • Drafts letters and plain-language summaries

What DOC never does

  • Assess symptoms or suggest a diagnosis
  • Give medical advice or tell you which treatment to have
  • Send anything on your behalf
  • Rank a clinic higher because it paid
