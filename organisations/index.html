For employers and clinics

DOC is built for patients first. That is also why it can be useful to the organisations around them.

Employers

When a colleague is stuck on a waiting list, they lose working days and peace of mind.

DOC could be a staff benefit that helps people use the choices they already have in the NHS. We never share an employee’s health information with their employer.

Clinics

Independent clinics that provide NHS services need patients to know they exist.

DOC shows every eligible provider. We would also like to connect with clinics’ booking and record systems, so referral details arrive clean.

One rule we will not bend

Options are sorted by waiting time and distance. No clinic can pay to appear higher, and no option is hidden.

Choose “Partnership” or “Clinic system integration” in the form and tell us about your organisation.
