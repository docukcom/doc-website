For investors

DOC is at an early stage. Here is the problem, the first product and the plan, without inflated numbers.

The problem

At the end of September 2026 there were 7.38 million waits for consultant-led treatment in England (BMA analysis of NHS England data). Patients often have a legal right to choose where they are treated, but in a 2020 Which? survey of its members, 63% said they were not offered a choice (Which?).

The first product

  • England only, one or two specialties with long waits, such as orthopaedics or gynaecology
  • An explainer for the right to choose
  • Waiting time comparison from official data
  • A letter generator for the GP
  • A simple referral tracker

How DOC will earn

  • Individuals: a subscription, or a one-off payment for support through one referral
  • Employers: DOC as a staff benefit
  • Clinics: possible, but never in a way that changes how options are sorted

Where we are

DOC is in development and preparing early access. It is founded and led by Andrii Nikolyn. The company is not yet registered. The hard part is an accurate base of rules and data. That is why the team already includes a clinical advisor with NHS experience and a technical and AI advisor, and why legal review is part of the plan from the start.
